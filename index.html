Megh Steel SKU tracker

Every SKU in the RM tracker vsm stock plan carrying a schedule or stock, keyed on OD, ID, thickness, length, grade and cut type. Megh Steel runs a vendor service model: Tata Steel supplies it, and it supplies TVSM, Royal Enfield and HMSIL. Supplied to names which — the plan marks the RE and HMSIL sizes with a Megh- key prefix, and which of the two comes from the conversion-agent code that has bought the size (943210 HMSIL, 943211 RE). A size the prefix marks but no code has bought yet reads RE or HMSIL and answers to both filters. Schedule, stock and the OMS order figure come from that plan; sales from the sales dump; the sales-planning order column is the live order book from the three despatching plants, excluding lines marked c in the remarks column, opened plant by plant; the last two columns are long-length stock Tata Steel could still allocate. Click any figure for its breakup. BOP marks a bought-out part — a size Megh buys finished rather than converting, from the list supplied by the owner; hover the badge for the listed size, pieces and plant. An amber badge means the listed length was nominal and the SKU was matched on the nearest cut length, with the gap named. Cut type states what the plan's own FC/NFC column says — FC is fin cut, NFC is not — while the cut token inside the SKU key comes from the governed bucket so the plan and the stock frames match on it, so the two can differ on a SKU.

Megh Steel length-bucketing

Megh Steel buys length-specific sizes, so its governing mapping is the bucket plus the length — and Bucketting alone cannot supply it: the plan's own 056/0789/0788 columns name material codes Bucketting has never heard of. This is that mapping, built from the plan itself, and it is what lets an order for such a code reach a bucket. Codes not in Bucketting is the queue to clear — copy the table and paste it into the workbook as a length-bucketing sheet. A blank Bucket means the plan's own key column is empty for that size, so nothing governs it; a blank Material codes means the plan names none, and Plan note shows where it says a code is still to be created.

TVSM long-length coverage

Buckets are in ascending order. Click any metric to see its source breakup and calculation. Order logged is the live open order book from the three despatching plants — lines marked c in the remarks column are excluded — Jamshedpur on balance to despatch, Hosur and Khopoli sales on balance for production, and the stock-yard transfers on actual booked to production — pooled to the bucket and opened line item by line item against the plant that owes it. The month-named sales column is what TSL billed on that bucket in the last complete month, so a month in progress is never set against a full one; it opens the whole billing window month by month, split between the ancillaries buying direct and Megh Steel converting for TVS. It is TSL's own billing and so does not carry Megh's dispatch to TVSM, which the current-month Total sales column beside it does — the two answer different questions and are not meant to tie.

Order book lines missing from a tracker view

Not all of these are unmapped materials. Lines the planners marked c in the order book's remarks column are excluded from both trackers by instruction; they are listed here so every line in the book is accounted for, labelled Excluded on purpose, and sorted last — they need nothing done to them. Filter Shown on to hide them. Everything above them is a line absent from a view that should carry it, and Shown on says where it does appear — a Megh order on an unscheduled bucket shows on the Megh Steel tab with its full tonnage and is absent only from the long-length tracker, so read that column before treating a row as a mapping failure. Shown on: Nowhere marks the tonnage that reaches no Order logged column anywhere; those rows lead the table. The Cause column names the fix: No governed bucket — govern the size in Bucketting (the Hosur/Khopoli transfer sheet carries no material number, so those are matched on description alone). Bucket not scheduled — the long-length tracker's rows are the buckets carrying a schedule, so nothing is wrong with the order. SKU not in plan or Length not governed — add the SKU to vsm stock, or govern the length. One row per line, so the tonnage total double counts nothing.

Stock analysis

Physical plant stock from PLANT STOCKS as material line items, split by cut length and long length and ordered oldest first. High age is stock ageing beyond 60 days at month end. Plant 4731 cut length is reconciled against RFD 4731 on weight, split into the tonnage RFD matches and the tonnage it does not — the two always add back to Stock MT, so both columns total — with a verdict and a sentence saying what the difference is. The columns are blank wherever the reconciliation does not reach: every plant but 4731, and long length everywhere. SAP holds 4731 and 8406 in kilograms only, so those rows carry no piece count. Click any figure to list its batches with ageing dates.

Stock transfer plan · Hosur EPA (8406)

Stock transfers run from 0789, 0788 and 4731 into 8406. The plan holds 15 days of cover at 8406 for Neel Metal, Rajsriya, Sandhar Hosur and Sandhar Mysore, stated per bucket because one STR line serves whichever of them draws on it first. Cover counts ground stock earmarked to those customers at 8406 plus everything already on the road to it. 8406 cuts to order, so long lengths standing there count in full. Sending-plant stock is shown per plant, because an STR is raised on one plant. Click a tonnage to open it; “Copy STR list” gives the finished-goods codes, quantities, source and destination to place the transfer.
